Chapter Five

Three Hundred Fifty-Eight Days

Nintendo DS · 2009

Kingdom Hearts 358/2 Days, released on the Nintendo DS in 2009, goes back in time to tell the story many players had wondered about: what was Roxas doing before Kingdom Hearts II?

The answer is a quiet, heartbreaking story about three friends on a clock tower.

Roxas, Axel and Xion

Days follows Roxas from the moment he awakens as a Nobody and is brought into Organization XIII as its thirteenth member. He has no memories and a Keyblade he does not understand. Day after day, he is sent on missions to gather hearts, and after each one, he sits atop the Twilight Town clock tower eating sea-salt ice cream with his friend Axel.

Soon a fourteenth member joins: Xion, a quiet girl who can also wield a Keyblade. She joins them on the clock tower, and the three become inseparable. But Xion is not what she seems. She is a replica created from Sora’s memories, and the more she grows, the more she draws away from the sleeping Sora’s heart.

“Got it memorized?”

A title worth puzzling over

The odd title is commonly read as “three-five-eight days over two” — the days of Roxas’s life in the Organization, shared between two people. Like much of Kingdom Hearts, fans have debated it at length, and part of the fun is the debate.

Playing on the DS

Days is mission-based: players choose assignments from a list and complete them in compact, replayable stages. Abilities and equipment are managed on a grid-like “panel” system. A multiplayer mode let friends play as members of Organization XIII — a rare treat for fans of the black-coated villains.

The story ends where it must. Xion’s fate is tragic, and Roxas, angry and grieving, leaves the Organization. He fights Riku, loses, and is placed in the digital Twilight Town — exactly where Kingdom Hearts II began.

Where to see it nowDays is included in the HD 1.5 ReMIX collection as a remastered cinematic movie, rather than as a playable game.
