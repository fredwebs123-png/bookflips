Chapter Seven

Data and Dreams

Re:coded · Dream Drop Distance

Re:coded

Kingdom Hearts Re:coded began life as a Japanese mobile game in 2008 and was remade for the Nintendo DS, releasing in Japan in 2010 and in the West in 2011. Its premise is wonderfully strange: Jiminy Cricket’s journal, which recorded Sora’s travels, has been digitized — and the data has been corrupted by glitchy “bugs.”

King Mickey and his friends create a digital Sora to explore the journal and debug its worlds. Along the way, Data-Sora learns about a mysterious message hidden in the journal: “Their hurting will be mended when you return to end it.” It points to everyone who suffered at Xehanort’s hands and sets up the series’ next major goal: to save them all.

Re:coded plays with genres freely, mixing in side-scrolling platforming, shooting segments and even turn-based battles. Today it is included in HD 2.5 ReMIX as a cinematic movie.

Dream Drop Distance

Kingdom Hearts 3D: Dream Drop Distance, released on the Nintendo 3DS in 2012, puts Sora and Riku through their own Mark of Mastery exam. With Master Xehanort set to return, they must become true Keyblade Masters — and that means awakening the “Sleeping Worlds,” realms that were lost to darkness and now exist in a dreaming state.

The pair alternate as players control first one, then the other, through a mechanic called the Drop. They meet Dream Eaters — colorful dream creatures that come in nightmare and helpful “Spirit” varieties — and explore worlds based on The Hunchback of Notre Dame, Tron: Legacy, Pinocchio, The Three Musketeers and Fantasia, along with a fresh visit to Traverse Town.

Two friends, two paths, one dream — and only one of them knows the whole story.

The real Organization XIII

Dream Drop Distance reveals Xehanort’s true plan. He has been gathering thirteen vessels for his heart — versions of himself and others he has corrupted — to form the “real” Organization XIII. To forge the χ-blade, he needs these thirteen seekers of darkness to clash with seven guardians of light. Sora was meant to be the final vessel.

Riku saves him, diving into Sora’s sleeping heart, and passes the exam to become a Keyblade Master. Sora does not — but his heart, and his determination to wake those who are sleeping, sets the stage for the final act of the Dark Seeker saga.

FlowmotionDDD introduced Flowmotion, letting Sora and Riku bounce off walls, grind rails and swing from poles. The fluid movement left a lasting mark on Kingdom Hearts III.
