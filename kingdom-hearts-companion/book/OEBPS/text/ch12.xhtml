Chapter Twelve

A Thousand Worlds

The places Sora has been

In Kingdom Hearts, each Disney film is its own world, floating in a sea of stars and kept separate by the “world order.” Sora and his friends usually try to blend in — which is why Sora becomes a merman in Atlantica, a lion cub in the Pride Lands and a toy in Toy Box.

Original worlds

  • Destiny Islands — Sora, Riku and Kairi’s tropical home, with its paopu tree.
  • Traverse Town — a nighttime refuge for people who lost their worlds.
  • Hollow Bastion / Radiant Garden — Ansem the Wise’s city, lost to darkness and later restored.
  • Twilight Town — an endlessly sunset-lit town, home of the clock tower.
  • Land of Departure — Master Eraqus’s training grounds, hidden as Castle Oblivion.
  • The World That Never Was — the Organization’s dark city.
  • Keyblade Graveyard — the barren site of the ancient Keyblade War.
  • Scala ad Caelum — a city of Keyblade masters, built from the old Daybreak Town.
  • Quadratum — the realistic city introduced at the end of Kingdom Hearts III.

Disney worlds, by game

Kingdom Hearts: Wonderland, Olympus Coliseum, Deep Jungle (Tarzan), Agrabah (Aladdin), Monstro (Pinocchio), Atlantica (The Little Mermaid), Halloween Town (The Nightmare Before Christmas), Neverland (Peter Pan) and the Hundred Acre Wood.

Kingdom Hearts II: Disney Castle and Timeless River, the Land of Dragons (Mulan), Beast’s Castle, Port Royal (Pirates of the Caribbean), the Pride Lands (The Lion King) and Space Paranoids (Tron), plus return visits.

Birth by Sleep: Enchanted Dominion (Sleeping Beauty), Castle of Dreams (Cinderella), Dwarf Woodlands (Snow White), Deep Space (Lilo & Stitch), Disney Town and the Mysterious Tower.

Dream Drop Distance: La Cité des Cloches (The Hunchback of Notre Dame), The Grid (Tron: Legacy), Prankster’s Paradise (Pinocchio), Country of the Musketeers and Symphony of Sorcery (Fantasia).

Kingdom Hearts III: Toy Box, Kingdom of Corona, Monstropolis, Arendelle, The Caribbean and San Fransokyo.

The Hundred Acre WoodPooh’s world is a series tradition: a storybook Sora reassembles page by page, filled with minigames and quiet moments. It appears in Kingdom Hearts, Kingdom Hearts II and Kingdom Hearts III.
