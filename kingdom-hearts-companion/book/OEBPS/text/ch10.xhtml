Melody of Memory

In November 2020, the series took a musical detour with Kingdom Hearts Melody of Memory, a rhythm game for PlayStation 4, Xbox One and Nintendo Switch. Players guide a party through stages set to songs from across the series, jumping, gliding and striking enemies on the beat.

Its story is framed through Kairi, who revisits her memories of Sora while searching for a way to bring him back. It also offers the first clear hints about where the story goes next.

The twentieth anniversary

On April 10, 2022, Square Enix held a 20th anniversary event and announced two new projects. The first was Kingdom Hearts IV, a new mainline game beginning the “Lost Master Arc.” Its reveal trailer showed Sora waking in Quadratum, a strikingly realistic city, and meeting Strelitzia, the young wielder from Union χ.

A new arc. A new world. A familiar boy with a key.

The trailer suggested a new look for the series, with a more detailed art style and a sprawling urban world that is very different from the Disney realms players knew. At the time of writing, Kingdom Hearts IV has not yet been given a release date.

Missing-Link

The second announcement was Kingdom Hearts Missing-Link, a location-based mobile game that would have used real-world maps and told a story set in Scala ad Caelum. After several rounds of testing, Square Enix announced in 2025 that the project had been cancelled, choosing to focus on Kingdom Hearts IV.

New ways to play the classics

Meanwhile, the existing games have kept finding new homes. Remastered collections brought the whole saga to PlayStation 4, Xbox One and, through cloud streaming, the Nintendo Switch. In 2024, the series arrived on PC through Steam, making it easier than ever for new players to start the journey from the beginning.

The collectionsHD 1.5 + 2.5 ReMIX covers Kingdom Hearts Final Mix, Re:Chain of Memories, Kingdom Hearts II Final Mix, Birth by Sleep Final Mix, and movies of 358/2 Days and Re:coded.
