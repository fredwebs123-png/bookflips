Appendix B

Glossary

For when the terminology gets heavy

Keyblade
A key-shaped weapon that can lock and unlock anything, including the hearts of worlds. It chooses its wielder.
Kingdom Hearts
The heart of all worlds, a source of tremendous power, often appearing as a heart-shaped moon.
χ-blade
The legendary original Keyblade, pronounced “key-blade.” Its forging requires a clash of light and darkness.
Heartless
Creatures born from the darkness in hearts. They seek to consume more hearts.
Nobody
The body and soul left behind when a strong-willed person loses their heart. Said to have no feelings, though many prove otherwise.
Unversed
Creatures born from Vanitas’s negative emotions, encountered in Birth by Sleep.
Dream Eaters
Creatures of dreams in Dream Drop Distance. Nightmares devour good dreams; Spirits fight them.
Organization XIII
A group of thirteen Nobodies led by Xemnas. The “real” Organization XIII is Xehanort’s later group of vessels.
Princesses of Heart
Seven maidens with hearts of pure light, including Kairi, Alice, Jasmine, Aurora, Belle, Cinderella and Snow White.
Guardians of Light
The seven heroes who face Xehanort’s thirteen seekers of darkness in Kingdom Hearts III.
Mark of Mastery
The exam a Keyblade wielder takes to become a Keyblade Master.
Dive to the Heart
The stained-glass dream platforms that open several games, reflecting a character’s heart.
Paopu fruit
A star-shaped fruit from Destiny Islands. Two people who share one are said to stay part of each other’s lives forever.
Gummi Ship
The customizable vessel Sora uses to travel between worlds.
Book of Prophecies
The books the Master of Masters gave the Foretellers, foretelling the future up to a point.
World order
The rule that visitors should not reveal the existence of other worlds.
