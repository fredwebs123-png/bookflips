Chapter Eleven

The Keyblade Bearers

The characters at the heart of the saga

Sora

A cheerful, stubborn boy from Destiny Islands who wins people over through sheer sincerity. Sora is not the smartest or the strongest character, but his heart connects to others so easily that it becomes the most important power in the series. Voiced in English by Haley Joel Osment and in Japanese by Miyu Irino.

Riku

Sora’s best friend and rival. Riku’s arc — from falling to darkness, to walking the line between light and dark, to becoming a Keyblade Master — is one of the most carefully developed in the series. He spends much of the saga protecting Sora in ways Sora never sees.

Kairi

The third friend from Destiny Islands, and one of the seven Princesses of Heart, whose pure hearts contain no darkness. Kairi originally came to the islands from Radiant Garden as a child. Later games see her take up a Keyblade of her own.

Donald Duck and Goofy

The king’s court magician and captain of the guard. They join Sora to find their king and stay because Sora becomes their friend. Donald’s temper and Goofy’s gentle wisdom balance Sora perfectly, and the trio’s bond is the warm center of the series.

King Mickey

The ruler of Disney Castle and a Keyblade wielder trained by Master Yen Sid. Mickey is often a step ahead, quietly fighting the darkness from behind the scenes.

Roxas, Xion and Axel

Roxas is Sora’s Nobody; Xion is a replica formed from Sora’s memories; Axel is the Organization member who became their best friend. Their story of friendship on the clock tower is the emotional backbone of Days and Kingdom Hearts III. Axel’s original self, Lea, eventually becomes a Keyblade wielder.

Terra, Aqua and Ventus

The heroes of Birth by Sleep. Terra struggles with darkness and is possessed by Xehanort. Aqua, steady and protective, endures more than a decade in the Realm of Darkness. Ventus, the youngest, sleeps for years with his heart sheltered in Sora’s.

Xehanort

The series’ great villain, appearing across time in many forms: the elderly Master Xehanort, the young Xehanort, Ansem (his Heartless), Xemnas (his Nobody) and Terra-Xehanort. His goal, in his own view, was not simple destruction but resetting a world he believed was broken.

Friends from Final Fantasy

Leon, Yuffie, Aerith, Cid, Cloud and others fill out Hollow Bastion’s restoration committee and Olympus’s tournaments. Sephiroth appears as an optional, famously difficult boss in the first two games.
