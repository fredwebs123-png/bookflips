The Timeline

The story in chronological order

Release order and story order are very different in Kingdom Hearts. Here is the saga arranged by when events take place in the story.

  1. Kingdom Hearts χ / Union χ / Back CoverThe Age of Fairy Tales, the Foretellers and the Keyblade War.
  2. Kingdom Hearts Dark RoadYoung Xehanort and Eraqus at Scala ad Caelum.
  3. Birth by SleepTerra, Aqua and Ventus, about ten years before the first game.
  4. Kingdom HeartsSora leaves Destiny Islands.
  5. Chain of MemoriesCastle Oblivion, immediately after the first game.
  6. 358/2 DaysRoxas’s year in Organization XIII, overlapping CoM and leading into KH II.
  7. Kingdom Hearts IIOne year later: Nobodies and the Organization.
  8. Re:codedJiminy’s journal and the message to save those who are hurting.
  9. Dream Drop DistanceSora and Riku’s Mark of Mastery exam.
  10. 0.2 A Fragmentary PassageAqua in the Realm of Darkness, catching up to the present.
  11. Kingdom Hearts IIIThe final clash with Xehanort.
  12. Re MindThe final act from new angles, and Yozora.
  13. Melody of MemoryKairi’s search for Sora.
  14. Kingdom Hearts IVThe Lost Master Arc begins in Quadratum.
Which order should I play?For a first playthrough, release order is usually the best choice: the story was written to reveal its secrets that way. The HD 1.5 + 2.5 ReMIX collection, then HD 2.8 Final Chapter Prologue, then Kingdom Hearts III + Re Mind, covers nearly everything.
