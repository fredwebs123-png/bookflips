Chapter Six

Birth by Sleep

PSP · 2010 — and A Fragmentary Passage

If Kingdom Hearts II explained the past, Birth by Sleep rewrote it. Released on the PlayStation Portable in 2010, it travels back roughly ten years before the first game to tell the story of three Keyblade wielders: Terra, Aqua and Ventus.

Three friends, one master

Terra, Aqua and Ven train together in the Land of Departure under Master Eraqus. When Terra and Aqua take the Mark of Mastery exam to become Keyblade Masters, only Aqua passes — Eraqus senses darkness in Terra. Soon all three are scattered across the worlds, each with their own mission and their own worries about one another.

Players experience the story through three separate campaigns, one for each character. The same events look different from each angle, and the full picture only comes together once all three are complete.

The man behind it all

The villain is Master Xehanort, a once-respected Keyblade Master searching for the legendary χ-blade (pronounced “key-blade”). To forge it, he needs the clash of pure light and pure darkness. He splits the darkness out of Ventus’s heart, creating a masked boy named Vanitas, and manipulates Terra’s anger at every turn.

“Our hearts are connected.”

The ending is devastating, and it quietly explains half the series. Xehanort takes over Terra’s body, losing his memories in the process; this is the young “Xehanort” who later becomes Ansem the Wise’s apprentice. Ven’s damaged heart finds shelter inside a young boy’s heart on Destiny Islands — Sora’s. And Aqua, after saving Terra’s body, falls into the Realm of Darkness, where she is trapped for over a decade. Before she goes, she hides the Land of Departure... by transforming it into Castle Oblivion.

Command decks and Shotlocks

Birth by Sleep introduced a deck of commands — attacks and spells that can be equipped and combined into new ones — along with Shotlock special attacks, character-shifting Command Styles, and D-Link bonds with friends. Its Disney lineup leaned toward the classics: Cinderella, Snow White, Sleeping Beauty and Lilo & Stitch.

A Fragmentary Passage

Aqua’s story continues in Kingdom Hearts 0.2: Birth by Sleep – A Fragmentary Passage, a short episode included in the HD 2.8 Final Chapter Prologue collection in 2017. Players control Aqua as she wanders the Realm of Darkness, years into her exile, holding on to the hope that her friends are still out there. It served as both an epilogue and a preview of Kingdom Hearts III’s engine.
