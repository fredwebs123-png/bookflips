Chapter Nine

Kingdom Hearts III

PS4 · Xbox One · 2019 — and Re Mind

Kingdom Hearts III was announced in 2013 and released in January 2019 on PlayStation 4 and Xbox One — more than thirteen years after Kingdom Hearts II. It was framed as the conclusion of the “Dark Seeker saga,” the long story of Xehanort’s plan that began with Birth by Sleep.

Seven lights

Sora begins the game having lost most of his strength after Dream Drop Distance. His first stop is Olympus, where Hercules helps him start rebuilding. His mission is to find the “power of waking” and help gather seven guardians of light to face Xehanort’s thirteen seekers of darkness.

Across the game, the family scattered by Birth by Sleep is slowly brought home. Riku and Mickey rescue Aqua from the Realm of Darkness. Aqua leads Sora to a hidden chamber in Castle Oblivion, where Ventus finally wakes. And at the Keyblade Graveyard, the guardians face Xehanort in a battle that pulls together nearly every character and plot thread the series has ever introduced.

Thirteen years of waiting, and every thread pulled tight at once.

New worlds

Kingdom Hearts III featured mostly brand-new Disney and Pixar worlds: Toy Box (Toy Story), the Kingdom of Corona (Tangled), Monstropolis (Monsters, Inc.), Arendelle (Frozen), The Caribbean (Pirates of the Caribbean) and San Fransokyo (Big Hero 6), alongside Olympus, Twilight Town and the Hundred Acre Wood.

Bigger, brighter combat

Sora’s Keyblades could now transform into new forms — hammers, drills, boats, yo-yos and more. Attraction Flow called in Disney theme park rides as finishing moves, and Team Attacks let Sora combine with Donald and Goofy. The game also included Classic Kingdom, a collection of minigames styled after retro handheld LCD toys.

The ending

Xehanort is finally defeated, not just by force but by a change of heart. Sora uses the power of waking to rescue Kairi — and the cost is himself. The ending leaves his friends without him, and a mysterious final scene shows Sora in a modern, realistic city.

Re Mind

The Re Mind expansion, released in early 2020, retells the final act from new angles and adds a challenging Limitcut episode featuring data versions of the Organization. Its secret boss, a young man named Yozora, connects directly to the city and to the next chapter of the series.
