Chapter Four

Kingdom Hearts II

PlayStation 2 · 2005–2006

Kingdom Hearts II launched in Japan in December 2005 and in North America in March 2006. Many fans still consider it the peak of the series’ combat and the moment its mythology truly opened up.

A summer in Twilight Town

Remarkably, the game does not begin with Sora. It begins with Roxas, an ordinary boy enjoying the last days of summer vacation in Twilight Town with his friends Hayner, Pence and Olette. They eat sea-salt ice cream, chase rumors of the town’s “seven wonders,” and worry about the end of break.

Then things begin to glitch. Time skips. Strangers in black coats call Roxas by name. A girl in white appears in his dreams. Slowly the truth emerges: Roxas is Sora’s Nobody, the empty shell left behind when Sora briefly became a Heartless in the first game, and this Twilight Town is a digital copy built to hold him until he can rejoin Sora.

“Looks like my summer vacation is... over.”

Nobodies and Organization XIII

When Sora awakens, he learns about the Nobodies: beings left behind when a strong-willed person loses their heart. They have bodies and minds, but supposedly no feelings. Organization XIII, led by the silver-haired Xemnas, wants to complete Kingdom Hearts — a great heart of all worlds — so its members can become whole.

The game also untangles a mystery from the first. The “Ansem” Sora defeated was really the Heartless of a man named Xehanort, an apprentice who stole the name of his master, the real Ansem the Wise. Xemnas is Xehanort’s Nobody. And the bandaged stranger called DiZ, who has been helping Sora from the shadows, is Ansem the Wise himself.

Flash and flow

Combat in Kingdom Hearts II is fast and flashy. Reaction Commands let Sora pull off context-specific moves with a single button press, and Drive Forms let him fuse with his companions to become stronger — Valor Form for raw strength, Wisdom Form for magic, Master Form for mastery of both, and the radiant Final Form.

The game revisits worlds like Agrabah and Halloween Town and adds new ones, including the Land of Dragons from Mulan, Port Royal from Pirates of the Caribbean, the Pride Lands from The Lion King and Space Paranoids from Tron.

It ends with Sora and Riku fighting their way home together, falling into the sea off Destiny Islands and swimming to shore, where Kairi waits. A message in a bottle from King Mickey arrives soon after. The adventure, of course, is not over.

Final MixThe Final Mix edition added tougher bosses, including the infamous Lingering Will and data versions of the Organization, along with a secret movie that previewed Birth by Sleep.
