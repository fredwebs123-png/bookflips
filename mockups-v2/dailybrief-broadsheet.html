DailyBrief, as a dark broadsheet

S3, carried through the whole brief: every section from your screenshots (greeting, narrative, progress, counts, the rail, Needs You, Texts, Teams, Slack, the footer), the fold layout, and a lock-screen glance. One serif, one sans, one brass. The handoff for the build session is updated to this system.

Rules

No cards. Sections are separated by rules: 2px ink under the masthead, 1px #3A3A3A around the counts and under section heads, 1px #2A2A2A between rows. Serif for anything that is a number, a time or a note-to-self; sans for anything you read as a sentence. Brass is only for needs-you and your name.

Rail

36px capsule nodes, height = duration (1 h ≈ 96px). Inset grey with a brass icon = calendar event; solid brass = needs-you, placed at a time; cream = the next event; ring = a text at its arrival minute (optional). Gaps collapse to an italic serif line. Now is a cream dot with a serif time.

Foldtwo columns

Masthead and the four counts span the width; the greeting, narrative and rail take the left column; Needs You, Texts and Work take the right. A broadsheet in two columns reads naturally here.

  • Rail and Needs You are the same data: the serif times on the right are the slots on the left.
  • No cards means no nesting; the column gutter (28px) does the separating.
