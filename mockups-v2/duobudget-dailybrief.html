01 · Couples budgetDuoBudget

Two people, one flexible budget, and the question that comes up at every checkout: can we afford this today, and who's paying? The system answers both on every screen, and never makes either partner do arithmetic.

Rules

Partner colors are identity, not status. Status is a separate scale: teal pill = fine, amber = watch, red = over. Money is always a per-day or per-person number before it's a total. Every bar is split two-tone so "who" is visible without a tap.

Month in reviewclosing the loop

Projection, not just history: where October lands if nothing changes, and the one card a couple actually argues about: settling up.

  • Weekly bars stacked by partner against the even weekly budget; the last two weeks are dashed projections.
  • "Where it drifted" names the cause in one line, not just the percent.
  • Settle-up is computed from who paid vs the 50/50 rule; Request or Mark settled.

02 · Morning briefDailyBrief

A brief is read top to bottom, once, in the morning. So the whole app is one rail of time: the morning view puts today on it, the inbox slots replies onto it, and the evening view moves what's left onto tomorrow's.

Palette

Paper#F7F5F0
Ink#1D1B18
Plum#8B2F5C
Plum tint#FBF4F8
Night#1A1816
Night plum#E9B4CB

Type

Sunday Instrument Serif · day names, section heads free until 12:30 · italic serif for notes-to-self Instrument Sans 600 · events, senders, buttons

Rules

Plum means "now" or "needs you"; nothing else is plum. Everything with a time lives on the rail. Inbox items that need a reply are not a list, they're events waiting for a slot. The evening screen inverts to night ink so the day visibly ends.

Today Inbox Apps

Evening wrap9:20 PM

The same app, inverted to night ink. It closes the day in four moves: score it, carry over what's left, preview tomorrow, set lights-out.

  • Carry-over is one decision per item with a proposed slot (Mon 9:00); Move drops it straight into tomorrow's rail below.
  • Lights-out is computed backwards from the alarm, shown as a bar from now.
  • Sibling apps report in one line each; nothing to open.
