The timeline, in DailyBrief's own dark system

No plum. These use the system your brief already ships with: near-black, soft dark cards, tracked uppercase labels, white for progress, amber only for "needs you". Same narrative, same "47% of the day" bar, same money items and texts from your screenshots. What changes is that the Schedule card becomes a Structured-style rail, and the things that need you get a place on it.

Type

One sans. Greeting 30/600 with the name in secondary grey; body 14.5 at 1.5 in #C9C9C9; labels 11.5 uppercase at 0.18em tracking; tile numbers 30/600. Nothing italic except gap notes on the rail.

Rail rules

Nodes are 40px capsules, height = duration. Grey capsule = calendar event; amber = something that needs you, placed at a suggested time; white = now / next; ring = a text that arrived at that time. Gaps collapse to one grey italic line. Amber never appears anywhere else on the screen.

V2 · Everything on the railone surface

The brief becomes a single timeline. Texts appear as ring nodes at the minute they arrived, money items as amber capsules at suggested times, the Teams call as a white "next" capsule. The narrative shrinks to one line because the rail now tells the story.

  • Stat tiles gain a thin white mini-bar each (how much of that count is handled).
  • "Texts on" pill in the rail header toggles the ring nodes off for a calmer view.
  • Nothing is a separate list anymore; order is time.
Pick this if the brief should read like a log of the day rather than a dashboard.
