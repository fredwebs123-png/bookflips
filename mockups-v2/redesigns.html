01 · Couples budgetDuoBudget

The number people act on is a daily one, not a monthly one. The hero now answers "how much can we spend today" and shows whether the month is on pace.

What changed

  • Per-day allowance replaces the lump sum. $412 until payday is hard to feel; $82 a day is a decision you can make at the checkout.
  • Pace line. Month-to-date spend plotted against an even-pace dashed line, with a one-glance verdict pill (on pace / ahead / behind). Switching to You or Kaley redraws the line for that person.
  • Two-tone budget bars. Every category bar is split into Fred's and Kaley's portion, so "who spent the dining budget" never needs a tap. Over-limit spills show in red at the cap.
  • Dark navy ground so the single teal line and the two partner colors carry the whole screen; less card-on-card than before.

Borrowed from

  • Buddy"$36 left to spend per day" as the headline figure; household budget switcher
  • Copilotbudget pace line with a "$3,000 under" verdict label on the endpoint

02 · Morning briefDailyBrief

A brief is read in order, so the screen is now one vertical timeline with a "now" line, instead of three tabs you have to visit.

What changed

  • Timeline with a now marker. Schedule, replies and free time sit on one rail in time order. The next meeting is the only filled block; everything else is paper.
  • Inbox folds into the day. Emails that need a reply become dashed timeline items at the time you'd realistically answer them, so "to reply" stops being a separate list.
  • Free time is written, not computed. "free until 12:30" in italic serif reads like a note to self, and is the thing you actually plan around.
  • Day name as the headline. A large serif "Sunday" with the date as an eyebrow; the greeting sentence is gone, the three counts stay as small pills.
  • "My apps" is a one-line scrolling strip at the foot, pulling the headline number from each sibling app.

Borrowed from

  • Structuredvertical timeline, current-time marker, "task nears, 3m" relative nudges
  • Tiimoserif day name over a week strip; time-of-day grouping with counts

03 · Store ops checklistOpsCheck

A shift check is a clock problem. The redesign puts the four windows on one rail with a countdown, and keeps only the open tasks in front of whoever is on shift.

What changed

  • Shift rail. Open, Midday, Afternoon, Close as nodes on a line, with the current window in amber and "42 min left" under it. The previous four equal boxes didn't say what was next or how urgent.
  • Done tasks collapse. Checked items drop into a "3 done · Show" group at the bottom, so the list shrinks as the check progresses. Tap a done item to reopen it.
  • Requirements live on the task. "Photo required" chips and an inline °F field for the roller grill replace the hint text; the submit button stays grey until everything, including the temp, is in.
  • Six-segment progress instead of a smooth bar: there are six tasks, so the bar shows six.
  • Who's on shift is a chip in the header, so a manager reading the log later sees it.

  • Todoerycollapsible "Completed Today" group under the active list
  • Business Insider · Action Pack"Due by" tags on tasks, green Done badges, daily/weekly sections with x/y counts
  • Skylightper-person "1/2 tasks done" with the person's avatar in the header

04 · GLP-1 weight trackerRetaTrack

For someone on a weekly injection, the week has a shape: dose, peak, fade. The redesign leads with the dose cycle and uses it to explain the rest of the day.

What changed

  • Dose countdown hero. "1 day 14 h" with a seven-notch cycle bar, the dose, and the drug name. The old "Next shot · 2 mg" row was the most important line on the screen and the smallest.
  • Injection-site rotation. A 2×2 abdomen grid shows the last site and the next one, and the Log shot button sits beside it. This is the one thing every dedicated GLP-1 app has that a general tracker lacks.
  • Estimated level curve. A sawtooth over two weeks with a dashed projection to Tuesday, and a one-line insight tied to where you are in the cycle ("level at weekly low, appetite strongest").
  • Weight chart gets a goal line (185) and a pace projection, so the −10.4 lb badge has somewhere to point.
  • Food, water and steps compress to three tiles with a mini-bar each; the macros breakdown moves to the Food tab.
