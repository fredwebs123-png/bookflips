Rules

Everything is a line item: label, dotted leader, value. No cards; sections are separated by rules (dashed between, solid at the top, double for totals). The one white surface is a "slip": the thing being printed right now. Partner colors are two inks; status is ink weight and a ▲.

Idioms

Tear-off edges on slips, *** END OF OCTOBER *** footers, F/K as stamped boxes, a square ink plus button instead of a round FAB. The keypad is a ruled grid, like a calculator from the till.

Add purchasethe slip being printed

The form is a white slip with a tear-off edge: as you type, the lines print. Amount at the top right like a till, then Where / Paid by / Split as typed fields, then the computed lines.

  • Who-owes-whom and the balance after are literally line items with leaders: "KALEY OWES FRED ...... +24.05".
  • Budget impact prints as its own section with an amber bar and a plain sentence: "$12 of this budget remains".
  • Keypad is a ruled grid; the Add bar is ink, with the amount echoed on the right.

The review becomes a statement: totals with a double rule, weekly spend as inked bars against a dashed "even week" tick, drift as line items, and settle-up as a boxed sub-receipt.
